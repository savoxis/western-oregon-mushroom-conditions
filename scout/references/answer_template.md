# Answer Template for Oregon Mushroom Scout

## Format

[HEADED WHERE] one line restating parsed location + radius + date + data timestamp

[TOP CALL] the single best zone: name, distance, species, score label, the 2-3 numbers that drive it, elevation band and aspect to work, habitat line, permit line

[ALSO IN RANGE] 2-4 more zones, one short block each: zone - species (score label) - key reason - permit note

[BURNS] (morel season only) eligible fires within radius: name, year, acres, distance, elevation band to work, snag + closure warning. If none: skip section.

[WATCH] what the next 10 days do to these scores

[FINE PRINT] ID disclaimer + permit reminder (always, verbatim from section 11 of the plan)

## Example

User: "I'm in Bend, it's May 24, anything close?"

> Working from Bend, 120 km radius, data as of May 24 7am.
>
> Top call: Metolius / Green Ridge, ~40 min NW. Natural morels are peaking -
> soil hit 51 F this week, 1.4 in of rain in the last 14 days, and the 3100-3500 ft
> benches went snow-free around May 8, which puts you 16 days post-melt, dead
> center of the window. Work mixed conifer north of Camp Sherman, 3100-3800 ft,
> north aspects now that the season is mid-late at this elevation. Deschutes NF:
> free-use permit required (free at the Sisters RD), 2 gal/day, cut them in half
> lengthwise in the field.
>
> Also in range: Santiam Pass (morels "worth a look" - crest is only 9 days
> post-melt, early but south aspects will have the first flush); Cascade Lakes
> ("marginal" - still snow above 4800 ft, give it 2 weeks); spring kings at
> Green Ridge are "worth a look" and will improve behind the morels.
>
> No qualifying burns from last season inside your radius.
>
> Watch: dry and 80 F forecast next week - that cooks the sub-3500 ft morels.
> If you can only go once, go before Thursday.
>
> Never eat any wild mushroom you have not positively identified yourself - scores predict fruiting conditions, not what is actually in your basket. When in doubt, throw it out, or run finds past the Cascade Mycological Society (Eugene) or Central Oregon Mushroom Club (Bend). Check permit rules for the land unit before picking; rules change.
