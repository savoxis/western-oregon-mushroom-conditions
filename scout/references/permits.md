# Mushroom Harvesting Permits and Rules - Oregon

**Compiled: 2026-08-14**

## National Forests

### Deschutes National Forest
**Last verified: 2026-08-14**
**Rule:** Free-use permit REQUIRED (no charge). 2 gal/day, maximum 10 days/year. Must be 18+, carry permit and harvest area map. All free-use mushrooms must be cut in half lengthwise at time of harvest. Matsutake has a separate paid season.
Source: fs.usda.gov/r06/deschutes/permits/mushroom-harvesting

### Willamette National Forest
**Last verified: 2026-08-14**
**Rule:** Up to 1 gal/day incidental harvest, no permit required. Free-use permit available for 2 gal/day, 10 days/year.
Source: fs.usda.gov/r06/willamette/forest-products/mushroom-permits

### Umpqua National Forest
**Last verified: 2026-08-14**
**Rule:** Up to 1 gal/day incidental harvest, no permit required. Free-use permit available for 2 gal/day, 10 days/year.
Source: fs.usda.gov/r06/umpqua/forest-products

### Fremont-Winema National Forest
**Last verified: 2026-08-14**
**Rule:** Up to 1 gal/day incidental harvest, no permit required. Free-use permit available for 2 gal/day, 10 days/year.
Source: fs.usda.gov/r06/fremont-winema/forest-products

### Rogue River-Siskiyou National Forest
**Last verified: 2026-08-14**
**Rule:** CONFIRMED DIFFERENT FROM OTHER OREGON FORESTS - matsutake has no free-use
tier at all: "a permit must be obtained prior to removing any amount of American
Matsutake mushrooms," fee and quantity not published online. For general edible
mushrooms (morels, chanterelles, boletes) the personal-use gallon limit could NOT
be confirmed from a live USFS page as of this date - do not assume the 1 gal/2 gal
pattern from Deschutes/Willamette/Umpqua/Fremont-Winema carries over here. Call
ahead: Supervisor's Office (541) 618-2200, or the specific ranger district for
your zone (Siskiyou Mountains RD covers Ashland/Applegate, Wild Rivers RD covers
Illinois Valley, Gold Beach/Powers RD covers the south coast).
Source: fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/permits/mushrooms-including-matsutakes (matsutake rule only - confirmed live)

### Siuslaw National Forest
**Last verified: 2026-08-14**
**Rule:** Up to 1 gal personal use, no permit required. More than 1 gal requires a permit.
Source: fs.usda.gov/r06/siuslaw/forest-products

## Bureau of Land Management (BLM)

### Coos Bay BLM
**Last verified: 2026-08-14**
**Rule:** Less than 1 gal personal use, no permit required.

## State Forests and Parks

### Oregon State Forests
**Last verified: 2026-08-14**
**Rule:** 1 gal or less per vehicle, no permit required.

### Oregon State Parks
**Last verified: 2026-08-14**
**Rule:** Up to 5 gal/person/day for personal use.

## Special Areas

### Crater Lake National Park
**Last verified: 2026-08-14**
**Rule:** NO mushroom collecting allowed. Full stop. The Diamond Lake / Lemolo zone borders the park - stay outside the park boundary.
Source: nps.gov/crla

### Wilderness Areas
**Last verified: 2026-08-14**
**Rule:** Personal use generally allowed; commercial collecting prohibited. Motorized access rules still apply.
